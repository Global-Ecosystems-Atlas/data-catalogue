## Abstract
A map showing terrestial and aquatic ecosystems was developed by combining different layers of spatial data. Wetland, river and terrestrial ecosystem data sets were used to build the most detailed national map of ecosystem types available to date for Malawi. Three main ecosystem types (aquatic - lakes, rivers and streams; wetlands and marshes; and terrestrial) were identified.
