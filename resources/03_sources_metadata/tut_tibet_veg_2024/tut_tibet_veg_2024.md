## Abstract
A new region vegetation mapping method based on terrain-climate-remote sensing was developed in this study, supported by the GEE (Google Earth Engine) and the random forest algorithm, combined with terrain, climate, and Landsat remote sensing image data, a detailed vegetation mapping of the Tibetan Plateau was completed at a resolution of 30 meters from 1990 to 2020. The overall accuracy and Kappa coefficient reached 95.00% and 0.95, respectively. The data includes vegetation maps of the Tibetan Plateau (in TIFF format, 4 periods, each spanning 10 years ), classification, and post-processing codes. This dataset can provide a basis for dynamic monitoring of vegetation on the Tibetan Plateau.
