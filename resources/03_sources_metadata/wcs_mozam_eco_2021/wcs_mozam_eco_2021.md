## Abstract
To develop a map of the ecosystems of Mozambique, we used a supervised and expert delineated approach, although this approach was broken up into three phases, where in phase 1 there was a reliance upon machine learning algorithms to allocate all areas to one of the proposed initial vegetation types. The naming and initial classification hierarchy was based on a strict vegetation classification.

The ecosystem map was therefore based on the vegetation classes, and when cross-walked with the IUCN Typology, resulted in 162 ecosystems, which resulted in 6 realms (level 1), 8 biomes (level 2), 15 functional ecosystem types (level 3), 32 regional ecosystems (level 4), and 162 ecosystems (level 5).

For the purposes of the Global Ecosystems Atlas, anthropogenic areas obtained from the Mozambique land cover map were overlayed and masked out so that the map can represent current extent.
