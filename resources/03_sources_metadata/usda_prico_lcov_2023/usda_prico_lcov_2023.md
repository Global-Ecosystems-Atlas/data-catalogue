## Abstract
This data publication contains one spatial land cover raster dataset, provided in multiple formats, representing land cover and forest formations circa 2000 for Puerto Rico, Vieques and Culebra. This raster dataset differentiates cloud forest into two classes and was created from previously published Landsat satellite image mosaics dated 1991 and 2000 for Puerto Rico, Vieques and Culebra. Also included is a land cover map showing the distributions of land cover and forest formations for Puerto Rico, Vieques and Culebra that do not differentiate cloud forest into two classes. Two images are also provided that show the land cover and forest formations that 1) do differentiate cloud forest into two classes and 2) do not differentiate cloud forest into two classes.
